Understanding Jaw Clicking, Jaw Pain & Limited Mouth Opening

Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) symptoms can appear as clicking or popping during chewing, morning jaw stiffness, teeth clenching, or difficulty opening the mouth comfortably. An evaluation examines both joint mechanics and bite forces before recommending a tailored care plan.

Braces vs. Clear Aligners: How an Orthodontist Plans Bite Correction

Both fixed braces and removable clear aligners can straighten teeth, but choosing between them depends on crowding, bite mechanics, and daily wear habits evaluated by an M.D.S. Orthodontist.

What Full-Mouth Rehabilitation & Dental Implants Involve

When teeth are worn, fractured, or missing, prosthodontic planning focuses on restoring chewing comfort, bite height, and natural aesthetics in carefully phased stages.

TMJ & Bite Care

Teeth Grinding & Clenching: Early Signs to Watch For

Flattened tooth edges, morning jaw fatigue, temple tension, or unexplained tooth sensitivity can point to nocturnal clenching or bite overload that benefits from a clinical check.
